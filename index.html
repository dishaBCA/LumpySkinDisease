KNOW THE DISEASE

What is Lumpy Skin Disease?

Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD) is a viral disease that affects cattle. It can cause fever, raised skin nodules, swollen lymph nodes and a drop in milk production. It spreads mainly through biting insects and can affect animal health and farm livelihoods.

If you notice unusual lumps, fever or other concerning changes, contact a qualified veterinarian promptly.

SIGNS TO WATCH FOR

Common warning signs

These signs can have different causes. A veterinary examination is needed to confirm the cause.

🌡️

Fever

The animal may have an elevated temperature and appear unwell.

🔴

Skin nodules

Raised, firm lumps may appear on the skin and body.

🥛

Lower milk yield

Illness and stress can affect appetite and milk production.
